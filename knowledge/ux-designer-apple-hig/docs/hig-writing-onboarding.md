# Writing and onboarding

Sources (read 2026-11-26):
- Writing: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing
- Onboarding: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding

## Writing (quoted)
- "Determine your app's voice." "Match your tone to the context." In a storm, the tone is calm and direct.
- "Write for everyone … Choose simple, plain language … avoiding jargon."
- "Consider each screen's purpose … put the most important information first."
- "Be action oriented … When labeling buttons and links, it's almost always best to use a verb."
- "Build language patterns. Consistency builds familiarity."
- "Adopt capitalization rules … Title case is generally considered formal, while sentence case" is more casual. Hazy uses sentence case.

## Onboarding (quoted)
- "Teach through interactivity."
- "Postpone nonessential setup flows … Provide reasonable default settings so most people can immediately start." → first run =
  one screen: search a place or "Use my location". No tour.
- "Avoid displaying licensing details within your onboarding flow." → Open-Meteo attribution lives in Settings → About and the footer.
