# Official safety messaging Hazy may quote

Source: https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-turn-around-dont-drown (read 2026-11-26)

- "A mere 6 inches of fast-moving flood water can knock over an adult."
- "It takes just 12 inches of rushing water to carry away most cars and just 2 feet of rushing water can carry away
  SUVs and trucks."
- CDC: "over half of all flood-related drownings occur when a vehicle is driven into hazardous flood water."
- Core advice: "It is NEVER safe to drive or walk into flood waters." Slogan: **Turn Around Don't Drown®**.

Use these verbatim (with attribution "National Weather Service") on High/Extreme flood screens. Do not invent other
safety statistics.
